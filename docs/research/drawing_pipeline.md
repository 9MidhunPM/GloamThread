# Drawing-derived characters and textures: first offline experiment

Research note, 2026-10-25. Inspected checkout `b96ad0e`; this is a research proposal, with no experiment results or runtime changes. See the coordinating [preparation brief](../DRAWING_PREPARATION.md).

The next useful question is **whether a constrained sprite preserves the identity of someone's drawing at the game's actual size**. Test that before choosing an interpretation provider. The [roadmap](../GENERATION_ROADMAP.md) already orders templates before generated animation, but leaves the input policy, sample corpus and recognition threshold unspecified. Its Phase 1/human-playtest prerequisites remain prerequisites for integration; research does not establish their completion.

**Confirmed current boundary.** The manifest contains 47 original assets. Couriers have 32 frames in a 288×208 atlas: 32×48 cells, two transparent pixels of padding per side, origin `(16,43)`. Terrain has 48 ordered 16×16 cells in 160×120 atlases. Python validates canonical terrain alpha masks and agreement between themes. The courier generator draws two particular silhouettes; it is not yet a reusable accessory rig. The content validator accepts exactly `tavi_fixed` and `neri_fixed`, with fixed specials/hearts/themes. A generic personal-character profile is therefore future work, despite the useful existing manifest boundary. Sources: [manifest](../../assets/manifest.json), [generator](../../tools/gen/art.py#L29), [profile checks](../../tools/validate_content.py#L144), [mask checks](../../tools/validate_assets.py#L265).

**Proposed experiment.** Use 12 consenting, original drawings from at least four contributors: four photographed pencil/pen drawings, four flat-colour digital drawings and four transparent PNG drawings. Across those groups include simple humanoids, broad/narrow silhouettes, large ears/horns, a plant/cloth motif and at least two difficult nonhumanoid designs. Reserve four drawings, including a difficult one, until the template choices are frozen. This is a feasibility sample, not evidence of general recognition accuracy.

For each drawing, its author names three defining cues before seeing the result. A human records those cues as bounded palette, silhouette/accessory and motif choices. Build an **appearance-only preview** from original project parts with the existing frame/pivot contract; preserve all mechanics. Keep the original drawing beside the result and record each lost cue or manual correction. A whole photograph reduced to 32×48 is not the proposed construction method. Unsupported shapes should produce an explicit limitation, not a claim of faithful reproduction. This isolates visual identity from model interpretation and balance changes.

Give three development drawings a small companion material board: modify only whitelisted interiors of `solid_fill`, `solid_fill_2`, `background_stone` and `background_cloth`; retain every other slot. Use one selected motif and a compact material palette. Composite within an authored binary edit mask, preserve canonical alpha and an initial two-pixel protected contour band, and freeze hazard/one-way/bridge/interaction art and shared danger colours. A matching alpha mask alone cannot prove a landing edge remains visible. This is a partial texture experiment, not an accepted complete theme. Pillow supplies masked compositing; the allowed edit regions and semantic checks are project decisions. [Pillow image operations](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/Image.html#PIL.Image.composite)

**Proposed local intake defaults.** Accept single-frame PNG/JPEG only, at most 10 MiB compressed, 16 million pixels and 4096 pixels on either axis. These are trial budgets, not library defaults. Restrict decoders with `Image.open(..., formats=...)`; reject oversized/animated inputs; treat decompression warnings as errors. Run `verify()`, reopen, then fully `load()` before accepting: opening is lazy, and verification alone does not decode pixels. [Pillow Image API](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/Image.html#PIL.Image.open)

Apply EXIF orientation, then contain the whole image within 1024×1024 without cropping signature features. Preserve alpha; do not infer paper transparency in this first experiment. `exif_transpose()` applies and removes orientation metadata; `contain()` preserves aspect ratio. [Pillow ImageOps](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/ImageOps.html)

Convert a valid embedded ICC profile to sRGB with `ImageCms`; retain alpha separately through colour conversion. Reject invalid/unsupported profiles explicitly; for untagged RGB inputs, record the assumption of sRGB. Write a fresh normalized RGBA PNG without source metadata. Retain raw inputs privately under anonymous IDs, with raw/normalized hashes, chosen cues and tool versions. Profile conversion can raise `PyCMSError`; a conversion failure must not silently become a successful normalization. [Pillow ImageCms](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference/ImageCms.html#PIL.ImageCms.profileToProfile)

Use Lanczos for photographic reference reduction and nearest-neighbour for pixel-art scaling; neither operation recognizes a character. Assemble final pixels directly in the target cell and constrain palette membership without introducing dithering noise. [Pillow resampling](https://pillow.readthedocs.io/en/stable/handbook/concepts.html#filters)

**Render and acceptance gates.** The current [SpriteAnimator](../../scripts/presentation/sprite_animator.gd#L15) already sets nearest filtering, disables centering and uses the negative manifest origin. Preserve full cells and this origin across poses; independently trimming frames risks visual foot sliding. Godot documents centering-related pixel alignment and explicit offsets. [Sprite2D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_sprite2d.html)

Keep lossless imports and no mipmaps for this fixed-scale 2D experiment. In Godot 4, filtering belongs to CanvasItem/project settings, not the old import filter checkbox. Use exact atlas regions excluding padding; `AtlasTexture.filter_clip` can guard against neighbouring pixels but does not validate gutters or pivots. [Image import](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/assets_pipeline/importing_images.html), [AtlasTexture](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_atlastexture.html)

Suggested decision gates, to agree before collecting results:

- **Mechanical:** every atlas satisfies frame dimensions/order/timing, origin, transparent padding and source/hash checks; material outputs preserve canonical alpha and all protected pixels byte-for-byte. Two offline rebuilds match decoded pixels. Invalid-file fixtures—truncation, unsupported format, oversized image, APNG, orientation and bad ICC—produce the specified rejection or normalization outcome. No runtime asset/profile replacement occurs in this research step.
- **Recognition:** four independent reviewers perform randomized four-choice drawing-to-avatar matching at native size, with similar-colour distractors. Require at least 40/48 correct overall and 3/4 for every drawing. Report the four held-out drawings separately. Each author must recognize at least two of their three cues. These are proposed stop/go thresholds, not measured claims.
- **Readability:** inspect all states/facings at 1× and 2×; play idle/run/slash/downed/revive in both 480×360 theme views. On a fixed set of eight scenes covering platforms, hazards, enemies and interaction markers, all four reviewers must identify each critical surface/target correctly. Any danger or player-identity confusion blocks that candidate, even if aggregate matching passes. Compare against fixed-art controls to separate a baseline problem from a new one.

Deliver the corpus index, normalized references, cue selections, full preview atlases, material boards, native captures and per-case results/correction time. If recognition fails, refine the supported shapes before automating interpretation. Passing would justify a narrowly scoped preparation prototype after the roadmap's prerequisites; it would not establish arbitrary drawing support, new build balance, generated animation quality or player enjoyment.
